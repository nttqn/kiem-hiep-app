Step into the martial world, join one of five sects, train, clear dungeons, be reborn, seek enlightenment through tribulation and rise to become the Supreme Master of the Wulin!

FIVE SECTS - FIVE ELEMENTS
- Kim Cang Tự (Metal): mighty staff arts and a huge health pool. The easiest sect to start with.
- Bách Độc Môn (Wood): throwing darts and poison, long range, high critical hits.
- Bích Thủy Am (Water): ice magic that slows and freezes, with self-healing.
- Liệt Hỏa Bang (Fire): blazing sabers, explosive damage, dash-in strikes.
- Huyền Thổ Quán (Earth): sword energy, earth shields and quakes that stun.
Metal beats Wood, Wood beats Earth, Earth beats Water, Water beats Fire, Fire beats Metal. Each sect has 2 schools to choose your own fighting style.

A VAST MARTIAL WORLD
- 13 training grounds: Lạc An plains, purple bamboo forest, Hoàng Sa desert, the Western Silk Road, Fire Mountain, the Eastern Sea Isles, Mount Hua's summit, the Nine Netherworld Abyss, the Sea of Blood…
- 52 kinds of monsters and 13 bosses with area attacks you must dodge.
- 21 three-star dungeons, 18 Nightmare dungeons, 11 fog-covered Secret Realms, an endless Trial Tower, the Demon Abyss, Inner Demons and Ancient Beasts.
- 56 main story quests plus 6 Reincarnation chapters; the Auto Quest button takes you straight to where you need to go.
- Bounty board, Treasure Maps to dig up loot, fishing with 36 species, seasonal festivals.

GUILDS AND FRIENDS
- Found or join a guild: daily check-in, donations, a weekly Guild Boss, guild shop and guild chat.
- Guild Wars: challenge masters of other guilds and earn War Merit for the weekly ranking.
- Make friends, send gifts every day and call a friend to help in hard dungeons.

COMPETE WITH THE WHOLE SERVER
- Arena and Sparring against copies of other players' heroes, with seasonal rank rewards.
- A weekly Martial Tournament for the title of Wulin Champion.
- A daily World Boss, a twice-daily Golden Boss, weekly Hunts and World Chat.
- Leaderboards for Power, Tower, Level, Arena and Bosses (sign-in required).

BUILD YOUR HERO YOUR WAY
- Level 99, 6 rebirths, then Enlightenment and Tribulation trials to keep growing stronger.
- 4 skills, 2 inner arts and 1 ultimate per sect; martial manuals, five-element meridians, Body Tempering.
- 7 gear slots: enhance to +10, reforge, socket gems, Golden sets, Awakening, Divine Weapons and Celestial forging.
- 5 companions, 6 spirit pets, 11 mounts (including a flying sword) with 14 mount skins, and 22 outfits.
- Rise through your sect to Sect Master, brew elixirs, grow herbs and raise silkworms on your own estate.
- Bag with filters, sorting and item locks. New heroes get a Legacy boost to level up faster.

GIFTS EVERY DAY
- Idle training: earn EXP and silver even while the game is closed.
- Login gifts, 7-day goals, activity chests, a seasonal Battle Pass, mailbox gifts and weekend events.
- 26 achievements that unlock titles.

PLAY ANYTIME, YOUR WAY
- Fight with a joystick and skill buttons, or turn on AUTO: your hero finds monsters, casts skills, drinks potions, spends points and equips gear.
- Most content plays without an internet connection (except leaderboards, guilds, Arena and chat). Low-spec mode included.
- Optional cloud save: sign in with Google or Apple to keep your heroes when you reinstall or switch phones. 5 hero slots.

TRUE WUXIA SPIRIT
- Lively chibi heroes, hand-painted companions and bosses, unique effects for every skill, rain, thunderstorms and night.
- Pentatonic music with zither, erhu, bamboo flute and war drums.

The game is in Vietnamese.

Free to play, with ads (mostly optional, watched for rewards) and optional in-app purchases.

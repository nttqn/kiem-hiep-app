Kiếm Hiệp Ngũ Hành - Notes for App Review

No account or demo login is needed. The whole game plays offline: tap
"Tạo nhân vật" (Create character), pick one of the five sects, enter any name,
and you are in the town hub. Signing in is optional and only used for cloud
save and the leaderboard.

About the game
Kiếm Hiệp Ngũ Hành is an original real-time action RPG set in Vietnamese
martial-arts fiction (kiếm hiệp), built around the Five Elements (Ngũ Hành:
Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth). All characters, monsters, scenery, effects
and music are drawn and composed for this game in code; nothing is licensed
or reskinned from another title. Content includes:
- Five sects, each with its own fighting style, 6 skills plus a sect
  ultimate, and an elemental counter system (each element deals +25% to the
  one it overcomes).
- 9 regions, 18 multi-wave dungeons with time-based stars, 56 story quests,
  daily quests, an endless Trial Tower, rebirth up to 5 times.
- Gear with 5 rarities and +10 forging, element gem socketing, 6 companion
  spirit beasts, five meridian channels, achievements and titles.
- Progression balanced with an automated play simulation.
The interface and story are in Vietnamese; the game's primary audience is
Vietnam.

Sign-in, data and account deletion
- Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In are offered side by side (cloud icon
  in the title screen or town, top right).
- Account deletion is in the app: cloud icon > "Xóa tài khoản và dữ liệu
  trên mây" (Delete account and cloud data). It deletes the cloud saves, the
  leaderboard rows and the sign-in account.
- Signed-in players appear on the in-game leaderboard with their character
  name, sect, level, combat power and tower floor (no email or account name).
  "Ẩn tôi khỏi bảng" (Hide me) in the leaderboard screen removes them.

Ads
- Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are always the player's choice (revive, double a
  dungeon reward, free potions). A rare interstitial may show when returning
  to town, at most once every 4 minutes.
- App Tracking Transparency is requested before the first ad request. Ads
  still work, non-personalised, if the player declines.
